Founder & CEO, Zbyte

Orakwe Chinecherem Michael, popularly known as MR BIG FSH, is a Nigerian digital creator and the founder of Zbyte.

Zbyte was created with a clear vision: to give everyday Nigerians a simple, transparent way to earn through digital tasks and referrals — built and operated from Nigeria, for Nigerians.

As Founder and CEO, MR BIG FSH oversees the direction of the platform, membership structure, task system, and the overall experience of every member.
