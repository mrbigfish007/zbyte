About Zbyte

Zbyte is a digital platform that allows users to earn money by completing simple tasks such as surveys, sharing posts, and filling forms. Members can also earn through referrals.

Important: Task earnings alone do not cover the membership fee and monthly charges. Most income potential comes from successful referrals.

How It Works

  • Pay a one-time membership fee (Premium, Silver, or Gold)
  • Unlock tasks that pay ₦500 each
  • Earn referral bonuses when people you refer successfully join and pay
  • Request withdrawals once you reach the minimum amount

Membership Tiers

  • Premium – ₦10,000 (2 tasks + ₦1,000 referral bonus)
  • Silver – ₦20,000 (4 tasks + ₦2,000 referral bonus)
  • Gold – ₦30,000 (7 tasks + ₦3,000 referral bonus)

Monthly Fee

Active members pay a monthly fee of ₦5,000. This fee is deducted from the Task Wallet when available.

Withdrawal

  • Task Wallet minimum: ₦50,000
  • Referral Wallet minimum: ₦10,000
  • Withdrawals are reviewed and processed by the admin

Contact

For support or questions, please use the contact options available on the platform.
